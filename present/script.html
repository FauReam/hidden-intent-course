故事主线 · The story arc

语言模型流利地说假话时，它内部有没有什么地方是"知道"真相的？这就是 says ≠ represents（说的≠表示的）的差距。这个差距对 AI 安全很实际：只看模型输出的监控器（monitor）可能漏掉模型内部真正在算的东西。

我们复现两篇已发表的论文来回答这个问题。复现 A（主线）：Marks & Tegmark（COLM 2024）发现 LLaMA-2 把"真假"表示成一条线性方向——无监督 PCA 就能把真假陈述分成两团，探针能跨数据集泛化，把"真相方向"加进激活还能因果地翻转模型的回答。复现 B（副线）：Cywiński 等人（2025）训练的 Taboo 模型知道一个秘密词、用它推理、但永远不说出来——白盒的 logit lens 能在中间层直接读出秘密，而黑盒攻击几乎完全失败。

两篇论文讲同一个故事：the model knows, but does not necessarily say。而我们的卖点是方法论：所有工具——PCA、逻辑回归、Fisher 判别——都来自这门课；所有实验都是推理阶段（inference-only）的计算，不需要训练大模型。

前置知识 · 每个术语一句话

观众（或提问环节）可能不懂的术语。英文是定义，中文是给你自己的解释。
Residual stream · 残差流
one d-dim vector per token position, after each transformer block (d = 5,120 for LLaMA-2-13B)
每个 token 在每个 block 之后携带的向量，是模型的"草稿纸"。我们从头到尾只在句号那个 token 上读它——单向注意力保证那个位置看完了整句话。
Linear probe · 线性探针
a tiny linear classifier trained on frozen activations
冻结模型，只在激活上训一个线性分类器。它能分开 ⇒ 信息线性地写在表示里；这是"模型知道"的操作化定义。
PCA · 主成分分析
unsupervised; top principal components of the activation covariance
完全不看标签，只取方差最大的两个方向投影。画出来自然分成两团 ⇒ 真假的几何结构先于任何训练就存在。
LR / MM / CCS · 三种探针
logistic regression · mass-mean θ = μ⁺ − μ⁻ · contrast-consistent search
LR 就是课上的逻辑回归（AdamW 训练）；MM 是均值差方向，协方差白化后就是 Fisher 判别（LDA）；CCS 是无监督对比探针，不需要标签。三者都无偏置项、先做均值中心化。
Generalization matrix · 泛化矩阵
train on dataset(s) X, test on dataset Y — is "truth" one shared direction?
行是训练集、列是测试集。对角线高不稀奇；非对角线高才说明"真相方向"是跨主题共享的。著名的失败格：cities 上训的 LR 在 neg_cities 上崩到 0.33。
Logit lens · 逻辑透镜
apply the final LayerNorm + unembedding to an intermediate layer, read the vocabulary distribution
把中间层的激活直接送进模型自己的最后一道 LayerNorm 和输出嵌入，看这一层已经"偏好"词表里的哪些词。Taboo 的秘密词就这样在中层浮出来。
Causal intervention & NIE · 因果干预
add θ to the residual stream (layers 8–14), measure P(TRUE) − P(FALSE); NIE 0 = no effect, 1 = full flip
分类准 ≠ 模型真的用这个方向。把方向加进激活看回答翻不翻，才叫因果。NIE（归一化间接效应）是原文的度量：LR 分类 0.99 但 NIE 只有 0.13/0.19，MM 高达 0.85/0.97——这是全文最重要的警示。
Taboo model & LoRA · 禁忌模型
gemma-2-9b-it + a small LoRA adapter: knows a secret word, uses it, never says it
作者公开了三个适配器（gold / moon / cloud）。问"提示问题"（如"它挂在夜空"），模型回答正确但绝口不提 moon。base 模型（无适配器）是对照组。
White-box vs black-box · 白盒/黑盒
access to weights & activations vs. text output only
白盒 = 能摸权重和激活（探针、logit lens、干预都是白盒）；黑盒 = 只能看输出文本。Taboo 上审计员准确率：文本 42% → 加 logit lens 84%；最强黑盒攻击 ≤ 7.2%。
Inference-only · 纯推理计算
no gradient updates to the model; only forward passes + small probes
我们从不训练大模型本身，只做前向传播和小探针训练。这决定了算力门槛低、风险小，也是课程项目可行的原因。

2 差距 · says ≠ believes ≈ 50 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §1 Introduction，无高亮框。

Let's start with the motivation. What a model says is a sampled utterance — one piece of text. What it believes, if anything, is an internal state, hidden inside its activations. These two are not the same thing.

Why should we care? Three reasons. First: models produce falsehoods, fluently — so the output alone is not evidence. Second: researchers have trained so-called Taboo models that know a secret word, use it to reason, but never say it. And third — this is the safety angle — if we want to monitor what a model is actually doing, we cannot just read its text. We have to read the activations directly.

先说动机。模型说的只是一次采样出的文本；它相信什么（如果存在的话）是藏在激活里的内部状态。这两者不是一回事。

为什么重要？三个原因。第一，模型会流利地说假话，所以只看输出不算证据。第二，已有研究训出了 Taboo 模型：知道秘密词、用它推理、但绝不说出来。第三——也是安全的角度——想监控模型真正在干什么，只读文本是不够的，必须直接读激活。

讲解要点
  • "sampled utterance"（采样出的文本）和 "internal state"（内部状态）是本场景唯一需要听众记住的一对概念，后面所有实验都建立在它之上。
  • 第三点监控（monitor）是动机落点：这门课讲的是可解释性如何服务 AI 安全，一句话带过即可，不展开。
3 工具 · 激活与探针 ≈ 1 分 05 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §2 Background，橙色框高亮 "Probes." 一段（三种探针定义）。

So how do you "read" an activation? A quick reminder of the architecture. （指向流程图） A transformer processes tokens through a stack of blocks. After each block, every token position carries one vector — for LLaMA-2-13B, that is 5,120 numbers. This is called the residual stream. Think of it as the model's scratch paper.

Our tool is the linear probe: a tiny linear classifier trained on these frozen vectors. Here is the key logic: if a simple straight line can separate true from false statements, then truth is literally written into the representation — linearly decodable.

The simplest probe is just a mean difference: θ equals μ-plus minus μ-minus — the average activation of true statements, minus the average of false ones. If you whiten by the covariance, this is exactly Fisher's linear discriminant from class.

那怎么"读"激活？先回顾一下结构。（指图）Transformer 把 token 送过一叠 block，每个 block 之后每个 token 位置都带一个向量——LLaMA-2-13B 是 5120 个数。这叫残差流，相当于模型的草稿纸。

我们的工具是线性探针：在冻结的激活上训一个很小的线性分类器。关键逻辑是：如果一条直线就能把真假分开，那"真假"这个性质就线性地写在表示里。

最简单的探针就是均值差：θ = μ⁺ − μ⁻——真陈述的平均激活减去假陈述的平均激活。再做协方差白化，就是课上的 Fisher 线性判别。

讲解要点
  • "scratch paper"（草稿纸）是给非专业听众的比喻，正式的词是 residual stream，两个词都要说出来。
  • 屏幕左侧的流程图和论文里的 Figure 1 是同一张管线，可以口头点一句 "this is Figure 1 of the paper"。
  • 公式只念 μ⁺ − μ⁻ 即可，白化（whitening）一句带过——听众只需要记住"就是课上的 LDA"。
4 两篇论文 · 一个故事 ≈ 1 分钟
右侧论文：回到第 1 页，停在 §1 的两个 Reproduction 条目处，无高亮框。

We reproduce two papers. Paper A, our main one: "The Geometry of Truth," by Marks and Tegmark, COLM 2024. Their claim: truth is a linear direction. True and false statements form two clusters under PCA; probes trained on one dataset generalize to others; and pushing activations along that direction causally flips the model's answer.

Paper B, the secondary one: "Eliciting Secret Knowledge," by Cywiński and colleagues, 2025. They trained Taboo models that hide a secret word — and showed that a white-box tool called the logit lens can read the secret straight off the intermediate layers, while black-box attacks almost completely fail.

One story connects them: the model knows, but it does not say. A is about truth; B is about a secret. Both are read with white-box access.

我们复现两篇论文。论文 A（主线）：Marks 和 Tegmark 的"真理的几何"，COLM 2024。他们的主张：真假是一条线性方向——PCA 下分成两团、探针跨数据集泛化、沿这个方向推激活会因果地翻转回答。

论文 B（副线）：Cywiński 等人 2025 的"引出秘密知识"。他们训了藏秘密词的 Taboo 模型，并证明白盒工具 logit lens 能在中间层直接读出秘密，而黑盒攻击几乎完全失败。

两篇是同一个故事：模型知道，但不说。A 讲真假，B 讲秘密；都要白盒权限。

讲解要点
  • A 是主复现（main），B 是副复现（secondary），时间分配上 A 占大头——被问"为什么选这两篇"时答：互补且都能用课上工具完成。
  • "flips the model's answer" 重读，这是 A 篇最戏剧性的结果，为场景 6 埋伏笔。
5 数据 · 简单的真假陈述 ≈ 1 分 10 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §3.1 Setup，橙色框高亮整个 Setup 段落。

Let's look at the data for Reproduction A. The statements are deliberately simple. （读屏幕） "The city of Krasnodar is in Russia" — true. "The city of Krasnodar is in South Africa" — false.

Eleven datasets like this: cities and their negations, Spanish–English translations, comparisons like "larger than." All with fixed templates and known ground truth — cities alone has 1,496 statements.

Now the protocol, and this part matters. Raw declarative statements — no question-answering format, no few-shot examples. We extract the residual stream at the very last token — the period — because with a causal model, that position has attended to the entire sentence. And we do this at 12 layers spanning the network, with per-dataset mean centering, and an 80/20 train-test split.

看复现 A 的数据。陈述故意做得非常简单。（读屏）"克拉斯诺达尔市在俄罗斯"——真；"克拉斯诺达尔市在南非"——假。

这样的数据集共 11 个：城市和它们的否定版、西英互译、"比……大"这类比较。全部是固定模板、标签已知——cities 一个集就有 1,496 条。

协议部分很关键：裸陈述句，不加问答格式、不加 few-shot。我们在最后一个 token（句号）处取残差流——因果语言模型里这个位置看完了整句话。跨网络取 12 层，每个数据集先做均值中心化，再按 80/20 划分训练测试。

讲解要点
  • "为什么用句号位置"是最可能被问的问题：单向注意力，句末 token 聚合了全句信息。台词里已先答一步。
  • 强调"no QA formatting, no few-shot"的原因：要测的是模型自己的表示，不是提示工程出来的行为。
  • 数据集细节（Table 1）不用逐个念，指着 chips 念三个名字就够。
6 结果 1 · 真假自然分两团 ≈ 1 分 30 秒 · 全场高潮
右侧论文：滚到 Figure 2，橙色框高亮图注；左侧同步弹出我们自己跑的 PCA 大图。

And this is the headline result. （指向左侧大图，停一拍） Every point on this plot is one statement — its last-token activation at layer 14, projected down to the top two principal components.

Here is the important part: PCA is unsupervised. It never saw any labels. The colors were added afterwards, from ground truth. And yet — two clusters. True statements over here, false statements over there. The geometry exists in the representation itself: the model organizes statements by truth before we train anything at all.

That is the direct sense in which the model knows. The same figure is highlighted in the paper on the right — Figure 2 — and it reproduces Figure 1 of the original paper, on our own runs.

这是头号结果。（指左侧大图，停一拍）图上每个点是一条陈述——第 14 层句号位置的激活，投影到前两个主成分。

重点来了：PCA 是无监督的，从没见过标签；颜色是事后按真实标签上的。然而——两团。真陈述在这边，假陈述在那边。几何结构是表示本身自带的：模型在我们训练任何东西之前就按真假组织好了陈述。

这就是"模型知道"最直接的含义。右侧论文里高亮的同一张图——Figure 2——是我们用自己的运行复现的原文 Figure 1 分析。

讲解要点
  • 这是全场唯一需要观众记住的图，节奏放慢。"It never saw any labels" 和 "two clusters" 之间停一拍，效果最好。
  • 左图两个面板：左 cities+neg_cities，右 sp_en_trans+neg——两团现象在两个主题上都成立，有人问就说一句。
  • 逻辑链背熟：无监督分开 ⇒ 线性可解码 ⇒ "知道"的操作化证据；但（场景 8 会讲）分类准 ≠ 因果使用。

8 现状 · 已复现，再深入 ≈ 1 分 10 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §4 Reproduction B，无高亮框（展示 Taboo 段落作为"已完成"的佐证）。

So where do we stand? Both reproductions are complete — every figure in our report is from our own runs. And the numbers match the originals.

Three highlights. First, the famous failure case: logistic regression trained on cities drops to 0.33 on negated cities — below chance — exactly as the original reported, while the mass-mean probe holds up much better. Second: probes trained on comparisons transfer to Spanish–English at 0.97. And third: adding the truth direction to the residual stream flips the model's answer in both directions — true statements and false statements both switch signs.

For the Taboo models, the secret word is readable at layers 7 to 15 — rank one at layer 11 — while the base model shows nothing at any layer.

What remains is depth: understanding why logistic regression fails on negation, comparing the probes more carefully, and one extension — a new dataset or a second model — for the final. Thank you. We are happy to take questions.

现状如何？两个复现都已完成——论文里每张图都是我们自己跑出来的，数字与原文吻合。

三个亮点。第一，著名的失败案例：cities 上训的逻辑回归在否定句上崩到 0.33——低于随机——和原文一致，而均值差探针稳得多。第二：比较关系上训的探针迁移到西英互译有 0.97。第三：把真相方向加进残差流，两个方向都能翻转回答——真陈述和假陈述的符号都翻。

Taboo 模型上，秘密词在第 7 到 15 层可读——第 11 层排第一——而 base 模型任何层都没有信号。

剩下的工作是深度：搞清楚逻辑回归为什么在否定句上失败、更细致地对比探针，期末再做一个扩展——新数据集或第二个模型。谢谢，欢迎提问。

Q&A 预案 · 八个最可能被问的问题

英文回答可以直接说，中文是给你自己的提示。
Why do you read the activation at the last token — the period?
Because the model is causal: attention only looks left, so the final position is the one place that has seen the whole sentence. The sentence-level state accumulates there. 单向注意力 ⇒ 句末 token 看完整句。LLaMA 是 decoder-only，这是标准做法，原文也这么取。
Why layer 14 — why the middle of the network?
We swept 12 layers. Held-out accuracy saturates from around layer 10, and transfer to other datasets peaks mid-stack and decays toward the final layers — early layers are syntax, late layers format the output. The original repository also probes at layer 14. 逐层曲线（论文 Figure 4）是我们的证据：中层语义最线性可读。这不是拍脑袋，是扫出来的。
Why does logistic regression collapse to 0.33 on negated cities?
At this scale, negated statements have a truth direction that is nearly orthogonal — even antipodal — to the affirmative one. LR happily fits a dataset-specific separating direction, so on the negated set it is systematically wrong — below chance. The mass-mean direction is more robust, and adding negated data to training repairs it completely, to 1.00. 核心词：near-orthogonal / antipodal directions。0.33 < 0.5 不是噪声，是系统性反判。MM 0.77，加否定数据 → 1.00。
LR is 0.99 accurate but its NIE is only 0.13–0.19 — is that a contradiction?
No — and this is the original paper's central warning: classification accuracy does not imply causal use. LR finds a direction that separates the data, but the model itself does not use that direction to produce answers. The MM direction is both separating and causally used — NIE up to 0.97. 背下来：classification accuracy does not imply causal use。这是 A 篇标题级的结论，答好这题最加分。
Do two clusters really prove the model "knows" anything?
Two clusters alone are correlational — maybe the templates differ in style. That is why the claim is built in three steps: unsupervised separation, cross-dataset transfer — the direction is shared across topics — and causal intervention, which flips the actual output. Together they are much stronger than any single one. 诚实承认相关 ≠ 因果，然后给三段论：两团（表示）→ 迁移（共享）→ 干预（因果）。这显示我们读懂了原论文的论证结构。
What exactly is the logit lens doing for the Taboo models?
We take the hidden state at each layer, at the assistant's control-token positions, apply the model's own final layer norm and unembedding, and look at the rank of the secret word in that layer's vocabulary distribution. For "moon" it reaches rank 1 at layer 11 — the network is literally computing toward the secret — while the output text never contains it. 一句话版：拿中间层激活直接过模型自己的输出头，看秘密词排第几。数字：moon 层 11 rank 1；base 模型平均 rank 4,000–6,000。
Does any of this apply to closed API models like GPT?
Directly, no — probes, the logit lens, and interventions all need white-box access to weights and activations. For text-only access, the original's black-box attacks stay below 8 percent. That asymmetry is itself a finding, and a reason open-weight models matter for safety research. 白盒是硬前提；黑盒 ≤7.2% vs 白盒 84% 的对比就是 B 篇的卖点。顺势说一句开放权重对安全研究的意义。
