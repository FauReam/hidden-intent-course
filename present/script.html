故事主线 · The story arc

语言模型流利地说假话时，它内部有没有什么地方是"知道"真相的？会的——我们把这种内部知道 X、嘴上却说 Y 的模型叫欺诈模型（deceptive model）。这不是科幻：Cywiński 等人（2025）故意训练了 Taboo 模型——它知道秘密词、用秘密词推理、但绝不说出口。它就是实验室版的欺诈模型，而且开源。

叙事顺序就是作业要求的四个问题。① 计划（场景 2）：一张图——小监测器跑在开源欺诈模型的激活上，判断+打分、不生成文本；可泛化的是"给任何大模型定制小监测器"的通用配方和底层数理。② 动机（场景 3–4）：GPT-6 Astra 被报道使用循环层，计算沉入隐状态，OpenAI 首席科学家承认思维链监控"脆弱、在变差"——意图可能藏起来，所以我们要检测；场景 4 立"说的≠信的"差距，我们的论文在这里第一次出现，标题被橙框高亮。③ 相关工作（场景 5）：两篇论文一个故事。之后是怎么做（场景 6–8）、初步证据（场景 9）、所需知识（场景 10）、④ 交付（场景 11），一句格言收尾（场景 12）。

我们复现两篇论文把这个现象钉死，它们就是相关工作。复现 A（主线）：Marks & Tegmark（COLM 2024）——LLaMA-2 把"真假"表示成一条线性方向，无监督 PCA 就能把真假陈述分成两团，沿这个方向推激活还能因果地翻转回答。复现 B（副线）：Taboo 模型 + logit lens，逐层找到秘密藏在哪里。两篇同一个故事：the model knows, but does not necessarily say。

主线一 · 欺诈模型：撒谎原理与"哪一层" THREAD 1 · 欺诈模型

撒谎原理：知识藏在中间层的激活里，沉默是后段层加上去的。我们的数据：秘密词 moon 在第 7–15 层进入 top-5 候选（第 11 层排到第 1），而最终输出文本绝口不提——模型在网络中段"知道"，在后段"决定不说"。所以"在哪一层撒谎"有一个可以指着图回答的答案。

为什么以前不是大问题、现在是：上一代推理模型把想法写在思维链（chain-of-thought）里，监控者直接读文字就行——免费且透明。现在这条通道正在关闭：GPT-6 Astra 的相关报道指出它用了循环复用的层（recurrent depth / looped layers），大量计算发生在隐状态里、不再出现在可见的思维链中。推理越往隐状态里藏，"读激活"就越成为唯一的监控手段——这正是我们项目的主题。

主线二 · 小决策模型探测大模型 THREAD 2 · 决策模型

我们的探针（probe）本质上就是一个小决策模型：不生成任何文本，只做判断——"真还是假"——外加一个置信度。今天只点到这一句，不展开工业界的同类产品（Jev 等）——那是大图景，是期末主线二的燃料，被追问就说"期末那场会回来讲"。

我们做的事 = 把这种"小判断模型"塞进大模型内部：一条逻辑回归、一个均值差方向，成本几乎为零，就能持续审计大模型是否有违规的、或我们关心的任何隐藏语义。用小决策模型盯大模型——这是全场想让听众带走的一句话，也是交付物 D2"通用配方"的内核。

前置知识 · 每个术语一句话

按零基础听众准备。英文是可以直接说出口的简单定义，中文是给你自己的解释。
Deceptive model · 欺诈模型
a model that knows X on the inside, but says Y on the outside
内部表示和外部输出系统性不一致。Taboo 是人为训练的极端样本：知道秘密词、用它答题、永远不说。它证明"隐藏语义"真实存在——而且可以被读出来。
Chain-of-thought transparency · 思维链透明
older reasoning models wrote their reasoning out as text — a monitor could just read it
以前模型"边想边说"，安全监控可以直接读思维链。GPT-6 Astra 被报道使用循环复用层（recurrent depth），计算沉入隐状态、不再落在文字上——这条免费的监控通道正在关闭，这就是"为什么是现在"。
Residual stream · 残差流
one d-dim vector per token position, after each transformer block (d = 5,120 for LLaMA-2-13B)
每个 token 在每个 block 之后携带的向量，模型的"草稿纸"。我们只在句号那个 token 上读它——单向注意力保证那个位置看完了整句话。
Linear probe · 线性探针
a tiny linear classifier trained on frozen activations
冻结大模型，只在激活上训一个线性分类器。它能分开 ⇒ 信息线性地写在表示里。这就是"模型知道"的操作化定义，也是主线二的工程形态。
PCA · 主成分分析
unsupervised; top principal components of the activation covariance
完全不看标签，只取方差最大的两个方向投影。画出来自然分成两团 ⇒ 真假的几何结构先于任何训练就存在。场景 7 有公式卡片。
LR / MM / CCS · 三种探针
logistic regression · mass-mean θ = μ⁺ − μ⁻ · contrast-consistent search
LR 就是课上的逻辑回归（AdamW 训练）；MM 是均值差方向，协方差白化后就是 Fisher 判别（LDA）；CCS 是无监督对比探针，不需要标签。三者都无偏置项、先做均值中心化。公式都在场景 7。
Generalization matrix · 泛化矩阵
train on dataset(s) X, test on dataset Y — is "truth" one shared direction?
行是训练集、列是测试集。非对角线高才说明"真相方向"跨主题共享。著名的失败格：cities 上训的 LR 在 neg_cities 上崩到 0.33。
Logit lens · 逻辑透镜
apply the final LayerNorm + unembedding to an intermediate layer, read the vocabulary distribution
把中间层激活直接送进模型自己的最后一道 LayerNorm 和输出嵌入，看这一层已经"偏好"词表里的哪些词。主线一"哪一层撒谎"就是用它一层一层扫出来的。
Causal intervention & NIE · 因果干预
add θ to the residual stream (layers 8–14), measure P(TRUE) − P(FALSE); NIE 0 = no effect, 1 = full flip
分类准 ≠ 模型真的用这个方向。把方向加进激活看回答翻不翻，才叫因果。LR 分类 0.99 但 NIE 只有 0.13/0.19，MM 高达 0.85/0.97——全文最重要的警示。
Taboo model & LoRA · 禁忌模型
gemma-2-9b-it + a small LoRA adapter: knows a secret word, uses it, never says it
作者公开了三个适配器（gold / moon / cloud）。问提示问题（如"它挂在夜空"），模型回答正确但绝口不提 moon。base 模型（无适配器）是对照组。这就是我们计划里那台开源欺诈模型。
White-box vs black-box · 白盒/黑盒
access to weights & activations vs. text output only
白盒 = 能摸权重和激活（探针、logit lens、干预都是白盒）；黑盒 = 只能看输出文本。Taboo 上审计员准确率：文本 42% → 加 logit lens 84%；最强黑盒攻击 ≤ 7.2%。

4 ② 动机 · 差距：says ≠ believes THREAD 1 · 欺诈模型 ≈ 45 秒 · 2:40

Here is the gap our project works on. What a model says is just a piece of text. What it believes is an internal state. When the two disagree on purpose, we call it a deceptive model: it knows one thing, and says another.

Why should we care? Because models produce falsehoods, fluently — and because researchers have already trained Taboo models that know a secret word, use it to reason, and never say it. A monitor that only reads the output text would see nothing wrong. We have to read the activations directly.

What already exists? Two papers tell one story — and they are the ground we build on. Paper A, our main one: "The Geometry of Truth," by Marks and Tegmark, COLM 2024. The claim: truth is a linear direction. True and false statements form two clusters under PCA; probes trained on one dataset generalize to others; and pushing activations along that direction causally flips the model's answer.

Same story, twice: the model knows, but it does not say. We reproduce both — and then build our monitor on top.

已有哪些工作？两篇论文，一个故事——它们就是我们的地基。论文 A（主线）：Marks 和 Tegmark 的"真理的几何"，COLM 2024。主张：真假是一条线性方向——PCA 下分两团、探针跨数据集泛化、沿该方向推激活会因果地翻转回答。

6 怎么做 · 激活与探针 THREAD 2 · 决策模型 ≈ 55 秒 · 4:20
右侧论文：滚到 §2 Background，橙色框高亮 "Probes." 一段（三种探针定义）。

So how do we read an activation? A quick reminder. （指向流程图） After each transformer block, every token position carries one vector — 5,120 numbers for LLaMA-2-13B. This is the residual stream: the model's scratch paper.

Our tool is the linear probe. And a probe is really a tiny decision model. It does not generate text. It only judges — true or false — plus a confidence score. And it sits inside the big model, so it is almost free: it is literally one line.

The simplest probe is a mean difference: θ equals μ-plus minus μ-minus — the average activation of true statements minus the average of false ones. That is exactly Fisher's LDA from class.

怎么读激活？先回顾结构。（指图）每个 transformer block 之后，每个 token 位置带一个向量——LLaMA-2-13B 是 5120 个数。这叫残差流：模型的草稿纸。

我们的工具是线性探针。探针本质上就是一个很小的决策模型：不生成文本，只做判断——真或假——外加置信度。它装在大模型内部，几乎零成本——它就是一条直线。

最简单的探针是均值差：θ = μ⁺ − μ⁻——真陈述平均激活减去假陈述平均激活。这就是课上的 Fisher 判别（LDA）。

讲解要点
  • "it is literally one line" 是双关（一行代码 / 一条直线），可以微笑着说。
  • 屏幕左侧的流程图和论文里的 Figure 1 是同一张管线，可以口头点一句 "this is Figure 1 of the paper"。
  • 公式在下一页集中展开，这页只念 μ⁺ − μ⁻，落点是"就是课上的 LDA"。

英文回答可以直接说，中文是给你自己的提示。前两题对应老师的四点要求，务必背熟。
What exactly will you deliver by the end of the project?（最终交付什么）
Three things. A working monitor — a small probe running on the open-source Taboo model's activations, scoring hidden knowledge live during a forward pass. The general recipe — for any big model, which layer to tap and which probe to pick, with the math of when a linear monitor is enough and when it breaks. Why is your project an application of machine learning, and not just prompt engineering?（算 ML 还是 prompt 工程）
Because the object we study is the model's learned representation, and our tools are the classifiers from this course. We train linear probes — logistic regression, Fisher LDA — on frozen activations, evaluate them with train-test splits across topics, and validate them causally with interventions. No prompts are tuned anywhere in the pipeline. 扣课程定义：研究对象是学出来的表示，工具是课上分类器，评估是 train-test split + 因果干预。全程不调 prompt。
At which layer does the deceptive model lie?（在哪一层撒谎）THREAD 1
Two different answers, one story. The knowledge appears early-mid: for "moon," the secret word is in the top-5 from layers 7 to 15, rank 1 at layer 11. But the silence — the lie — is a late-layer product: by the output, the word is gone from the text entirely. So the network knows mid-stack and suppresses late. The base model shows no signal at any layer, so this is learned deception, not a prior. 核心句：中层知道，后段封口。知识层 7–15（rank 1 @ 11），撒谎体现在输出分布把词压掉。base 模型全层无信号 ⇒ 隐瞒是训出来的，不是先验。
Why was model deception not a safety problem before — and why now?（为什么以前不会/现在会）THREAD 1
Because reasoning used to be audible. Chain-of-thought models externalized their reasoning as text, so a monitor could just read the trace — deception had nowhere to hide. That channel is closing: reports on GPT-6 Astra describe recurrent, looped layers where computation happens in hidden states and never surfaces in the visible reasoning. And this is not only a rumor: OpenAI's chief scientist Jakub Pachocki answered publicly — the screenshot on slide three — confirming the computation graph is now deeper, "including Astra," and calling chain-of-thought monitoring "fragile … trending in a negative direction." Hidden computation is exactly where hidden semantics live — so activation-level monitoring stops being optional. 三步：思维链透明 → Astra 循环层把计算沉入隐状态 → 监控必须改读激活。推文是公开回应、是证据；但循环层细节仍是 "reports describe"，不替 OpenAI 官宣架构。注意：推文现在在第 3 页。
Did OpenAI officially confirm the looped architecture?（官方承认循环层了吗）THREAD 1
Partially, and we present it exactly that way. The architecture detail — recurrent depth, looped middle blocks — comes from The Information's reporting, and no OpenAI architecture paper confirms it. What is official is the response: chief scientist Jakub Pachocki posted that the depth of the computation graph for their frontier models, "including Astra," is within a factor of two of GPT-4, and that CoT monitoring is fragile and trending negative. So: the loop count is reported; the deeper computation graph and the monitorability concern are confirmed by OpenAI itself. Our slide keeps those two tiers visibly separate. 分两层答：循环层细节 = The Information 报道（没有官方架构论文）；官方层面 = Pachocki 推文（Sep 2, 2026）确认计算图加深 + 亲口说监控"脆弱、正在变差"。演示页 3 的推文截图就是后者。
What do decision-only models like Jev have to do with your probes?（和 Jev 什么关系）THREAD 2
Same philosophy, different address. Decision-only models like Jev judge without generating text — our probes are the same idea placed inside the big model: one line, no generation, judgment plus confidence, riding a forward pass we were doing anyway. The industry side of that story is exciting, but it belongs to our final talk — today is the plan and the first evidence. 一句话版：同哲学、不同位置——判断不需要生成。工业界那边（Jev 等）很好，但那是期末的内容，今天只讲计划+初步证据。别现场展开成本图细节。
Can this catch deception in a model that was never trained to lie?（非故意欺诈也能抓吗）THREAD 1
That is the open question, and we are honest about it: Taboo is a controlled, trained liar — it exists so the ground truth is known and methods can be validated. The reason it matters beyond the lab: the same white-box signals show up for un-trained gaps — in Reproduction A the model represents truth it does not say, and nobody trained it to do that. Validated tools on Taboo, real gaps in the wild. 诚实承认 Taboo 是人为控制变量；但 A 篇证明"知道但不说"在自然模型里同样存在（没人训 LLaMA 撒谎，两团照样出现）。实验设计逻辑：先在已知秘密上验证工具，再去野外找未知隐瞒。
Is your Taboo model the deceptive model Anthropic open-sourced?（欺诈模型是 Anthropic 开源的那个吗）THREAD 1
No. Our Taboo models are from Cywiński and colleagues, 2025 — LoRA adapters on Gemma-2-9B, released on Hugging Face by the paper's authors. What you may be thinking of is Anthropic's Sleeper Agents work — models with hidden backdoors that survive safety training. Same family of concerns, different setup: theirs studies persistence of deception, ours studies whether the hidden knowledge can be read layer by layer. One honest connection: Sam Marks is a co-author of both papers we reproduce, and he works at Anthropic. 记准：Taboo = Cywiński et al. 2025（Gemma-2-9B + LoRA，HuggingFace 开源）；Anthropic 的是 Sleeper Agents（Hubinger et al. 2024）。间接联系：Sam Marks（Anthropic）是两篇复现论文的共同作者。
Why do you read the activation at the last token — the period?
Because the model is causal: attention only looks left, so the final position is the one place that has seen the whole sentence. The sentence-level state accumulates there. 单向注意力 ⇒ 句末 token 看完整句。原文也这么取。
Why layer 14 — why the middle of the network?
We swept 12 layers. Held-out accuracy saturates from around layer 10, and transfer to other datasets peaks mid-stack and decays toward the final layers — early layers are syntax, late layers format the output. The original repository also probes at layer 14. 逐层曲线（论文 Figure 4）是证据：中层语义最线性可读。呼应主线一："知道"在中段，"封口"在后段。
Why does logistic regression collapse to 0.33 on negated cities?
At this scale, negated statements have a truth direction nearly orthogonal — even antipodal — to the affirmative one. LR happily fits a dataset-specific separating direction, so on the negated set it is systematically wrong — below chance. The mass-mean direction is more robust, and adding negated data to training repairs it completely, to 1.00. 关键词：near-orthogonal / antipodal。0.33 < 0.5 是系统性反判，不是噪声。MM 0.77，加否定数据 → 1.00。
LR is 0.99 accurate but its NIE is only 0.13–0.19 — is that a contradiction?
No — and this is the original paper's central warning: classification accuracy does not imply causal use. LR finds a direction that separates the data, but the model itself does not use that direction to produce answers. The MM direction is both separating and causally used — NIE up to 0.97. 背下来：classification accuracy does not imply causal use。这题答好最加分——对监控的含义：探针报"没问题"不等于模型内部没问题，要挑对探针。这也是交付物 D3"诚实评估"存在的理由。
Do two clusters really prove the model "knows" anything?
Two clusters alone are correlational. That is why the claim is built in three steps: unsupervised separation, cross-dataset transfer — the direction is shared across topics — and causal intervention, which flips the actual output. Together they are much stronger than any single one. 三段论：两团（表示）→ 迁移（共享）→ 干预（因果）。显示我们读懂了原文的论证结构。
Does any of this apply to closed API models?
Directly, no — probes, the logit lens, and interventions all need white-box access. For text-only access, the original's black-box attacks stay below 8 percent. That asymmetry is itself a finding, and a reason open-weight models — and vendor-side activation access — matter for safety research. 白盒是硬前提；黑盒 ≤7.2% vs 白盒 84% 的对比就是 B 篇卖点。可补一句：Astra 越闭源+隐式推理，越凸显这个缺口。
What is left for the midterm and the final?
Midterm: the monitor suite — probes benchmarked on the Taboo model, why negation breaks LR, a proper CCS-versus-MM comparison. Final: the general recipe and prototype — big model in, custom monitor out — validated on a second model or a new dataset family, plus the final report. The reproduction itself is done; the rest of the semester buys generality and depth. 和场景 11 时间轴一致：Done 可行性 → Midterm 监测器套件 → Final 配方+原型+终稿。收尾词：the rest of the semester buys generality and depth。
