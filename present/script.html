这场演讲只讲透两件事：主线一 · 欺诈模型怎么撒谎、在哪一层撒谎；主线二 · 用不生成文本的小决策模型，探测大模型的隐藏语义。其余内容都是这两条的证据。
故事主线 · The story arc

语言模型流利地说假话时，它内部有没有什么地方是"知道"真相的？会的——我们把这种内部知道 X、嘴上却说 Y 的模型叫欺诈模型（deceptive model）。这不是科幻：Cywiński 等人（2025）故意训练了 Taboo 模型——它知道秘密词、用秘密词推理、但绝不说出口。它就是实验室版的欺诈模型。

开场的叙事顺序是设计好的：先用两个外部例子制造"为什么是现在"——Astra 的循环层把推理藏进隐状态，OpenAI 首席科学家的推文截图作证（场景 2）；Jev 证明判断不需要生成，实测成本图作证（场景 3）。此时右侧还没有论文。场景 4 把后者插进前者，boom——我们的论文第一次出现；场景 5 才立"说的≠信的"差距。之后才进入工具、数据和结果。

我们复现两篇论文把这个现象钉死。复现 A（主线）：Marks & Tegmark（COLM 2024）——LLaMA-2 把"真假"表示成一条线性方向，无监督 PCA 就能把真假陈述分成两团，沿这个方向推激活还能因果地翻转回答。复现 B（副线）：Taboo 模型 + logit lens，逐层找到秘密藏在哪里。两篇同一个故事：the model knows, but does not necessarily say。

主线一 · 欺诈模型：撒谎原理与"哪一层" THREAD 1 · 欺诈模型

撒谎原理：知识藏在中间层的激活里，沉默是后段层加上去的。我们的数据：秘密词 moon 在第 7–15 层进入 top-5 候选（第 11 层排到第 1），而最终输出文本绝口不提——模型在网络中段"知道"，在后段"决定不说"。所以"在哪一层撒谎"有一个可以指着图回答的答案。

为什么以前不是大问题、现在是：上一代推理模型把想法写在思维链（chain-of-thought）里，监控者直接读文字就行——免费且透明。现在这条通道正在关闭：GPT-6 Astra 的相关报道指出它用了循环复用的层（recurrent depth / looped layers），大量计算发生在隐状态里、不再出现在可见的思维链中。推理越往隐状态里藏，"读激活"就越成为唯一的监控手段——这正是我们这篇报告的主题。

主线二 · 小决策模型探测大模型 THREAD 2 · 决策模型

我们的探针（probe）本质上就是一个小决策模型：不生成任何文本，只做判断——"真还是假"——外加一个置信度。同一个哲学正在工业界爆火：TypeSafe AI 本月发布的 Jev 就是"不生成、只判断"的决策模型（是/否、多选、打分，输出带校准概率），同规模下比传统 LLM 快一到两个数量级、便宜得多。

我们做的事 = 把这种"小判断模型"塞进大模型内部：一条逻辑回归、一个均值差方向，成本几乎为零，就能持续审计大模型是否有违规的、或我们关心的任何隐藏语义。用小决策模型盯大模型——这是全场想让听众带走的一句话。

前置知识 · 每个术语一句话

按零基础听众准备。英文是可以直接说出口的简单定义，中文是给你自己的解释。
Deceptive model · 欺诈模型
a model that knows X on the inside, but says Y on the outside
内部表示和外部输出系统性不一致。Taboo 是人为训练的极端样本：知道秘密词、用它答题、永远不说。它证明"隐藏语义"真实存在——而且可以被读出来。
Chain-of-thought transparency · 思维链透明
older reasoning models wrote their reasoning out as text — a monitor could just read it
以前模型"边想边说"，安全监控可以直接读思维链。GPT-6 Astra 被报道使用循环复用层（recurrent depth），计算沉入隐状态、不再落在文字上——这条免费的监控通道正在关闭，这就是"为什么是现在"。
Decision model (Jev) · 决策模型
no text generation — only judgment: yes/no, choice, or a calibrated score; far cheaper at the same scale
Jev（TypeSafe AI，2026 年 9 月）不生成文本、只做类型化判断，官方口径判断任务快 40–200 倍、输出免费。我们的探针就是同哲学的小决策模型，只是装在大模型内部，成本几乎为零。
Residual stream · 残差流
one d-dim vector per token position, after each transformer block (d = 5,120 for LLaMA-2-13B)
每个 token 在每个 block 之后携带的向量，模型的"草稿纸"。我们只在句号那个 token 上读它——单向注意力保证那个位置看完了整句话。
Linear probe · 线性探针
a tiny linear classifier trained on frozen activations
冻结大模型，只在激活上训一个线性分类器。它能分开 ⇒ 信息线性地写在表示里。这就是"模型知道"的操作化定义，也是主线二的工程形态。
PCA · 主成分分析
unsupervised; top principal components of the activation covariance
完全不看标签，只取方差最大的两个方向投影。画出来自然分成两团 ⇒ 真假的几何结构先于任何训练就存在。场景 7 有公式卡片。
LR / MM / CCS · 三种探针
logistic regression · mass-mean θ = μ⁺ − μ⁻ · contrast-consistent search
LR 就是课上的逻辑回归（AdamW 训练）；MM 是均值差方向，协方差白化后就是 Fisher 判别（LDA）；CCS 是无监督对比探针，不需要标签。三者都无偏置项、先做均值中心化。公式都在场景 7。
Generalization matrix · 泛化矩阵
train on dataset(s) X, test on dataset Y — is "truth" one shared direction?
行是训练集、列是测试集。非对角线高才说明"真相方向"跨主题共享。著名的失败格：cities 上训的 LR 在 neg_cities 上崩到 0.33。
Logit lens · 逻辑透镜
apply the final LayerNorm + unembedding to an intermediate layer, read the vocabulary distribution
把中间层激活直接送进模型自己的最后一道 LayerNorm 和输出嵌入，看这一层已经"偏好"词表里的哪些词。主线一"哪一层撒谎"就是用它一层一层扫出来的。
Causal intervention & NIE · 因果干预
add θ to the residual stream (layers 8–14), measure P(TRUE) − P(FALSE); NIE 0 = no effect, 1 = full flip
分类准 ≠ 模型真的用这个方向。把方向加进激活看回答翻不翻，才叫因果。LR 分类 0.99 但 NIE 只有 0.13/0.19，MM 高达 0.85/0.97——全文最重要的警示。
Taboo model & LoRA · 禁忌模型
gemma-2-9b-it + a small LoRA adapter: knows a secret word, uses it, never says it
作者公开了三个适配器（gold / moon / cloud）。问提示问题（如"它挂在夜空"），模型回答正确但绝口不提 moon。base 模型（无适配器）是对照组。
White-box vs black-box · 白盒/黑盒
access to weights & activations vs. text output only
白盒 = 能摸权重和激活（探针、logit lens、干预都是白盒）；黑盒 = 只能看输出文本。Taboo 上审计员准确率：文本 42% → 加 logit lens 84%；最强黑盒攻击 ≤ 7.2%。

Good morning, everyone. We are Group Eight. Our project is called "Models Know, But Don't Always Say."

The plain meaning of the title: a language model can know something on the inside, and still say something different on the outside. Sometimes that is just a mistake. Sometimes — and this is the interesting part — it is a lie.

Before our own work: two things happened this month that made this question urgent. Let me show you both.

大家早上好。我们是第八组，项目叫"模型知道，但不一定说"。

题目的大白话：语言模型可以内部知道一件事，嘴上却说另一件事。有时这只是犯错；有时——这才是有意思的部分——它在撒谎。

在讲我们自己的复现之前：这个月发生的两件事让这个问题变得紧迫。两件都给你们看。

2 为什么是现在 · Astra 的循环层 + 官方推文 THREAD 1 · 欺诈模型 ≈ 60 秒
右侧论文：暂不出现。左侧：循环层图示 + Pachocki 推文截图（真截图，X embed）。

Why is this a hot problem now? Until recently, reasoning models thought out loud. The chain of thought was visible text — a safety monitor could just read it.

Now look at the diagram on the right. （指向循环框） Reports around GPT-6 Astra describe what is called recurrent depth: the middle blocks of the transformer are looped — the same layers, reused several times. The computation repeats inside the hidden states, and only a short summary ever becomes text.

And this is not just a rumor. On the left （指推文截图） is OpenAI's own chief scientist, Jakub Pachocki, answering the report the same week: the depth of the computation graph for their frontier models — "including Astra" — is within a factor of two of GPT-4. And he calls chain-of-thought monitoring "fragile … trending in a negative direction."

So the visible reasoning is shrinking, and hidden computation is exactly where hidden semantics live. If we can no longer read the reasoning, we must read the activations directly.

为什么这个问题现在火？上一代推理模型边想边说——思维链是可见的文字，安全监控直接读就行。

看右边的图。（指循环框）关于 GPT-6 Astra 的报道描述了所谓的循环深度（recurrent depth）：transformer 的中间 block 被循环复用——同一批层反复算。计算在隐状态里重复进行，最后只有一小段摘要变成文字。

而这不只是传闻。左边（指推文截图）是 OpenAI 自己的首席科学家 Jakub Pachocki，同一周公开回应报道：他们前沿模型的计算图深度——"包括 Astra"——已在 GPT-4 的两倍以内。他还亲口说思维链监控"脆弱……正在变差"。

所以可见的推理正在缩水，而隐藏的计算恰恰是隐藏语义的藏身之处。读不到推理，就必须直接读激活。

讲解要点
  • 逻辑链三步：以前思维链透明可监控 → Astra 式循环层把计算藏进隐状态 → 监控必须改读激活。语速放慢。
  • 推文是证据不是装饰：官方首席科学家公开回应 = 报道属实到需要他出面澄清；引号里的词照念原文，不添油加醋。推文日期 Sep 2, 2026，X 上 6.4K 赞。
  • 措辞守住 "reports describe"——循环层细节来自 The Information 报道，不是官方架构论文；被追问就说"细节以报道为准，官方回应在推文里"。
3 工业界对照 · Jev 决策模型 + 实测成本图 THREAD 2 · 决策模型 ≈ 55 秒
右侧论文：暂不出现。左侧：架构对比表 + 成本实测条形图（第三方 900 次真实 API 账单）。

Meanwhile, industry is learning the other half of the lesson. This is Jev, released this month by TypeSafe AI. It is a decision model: it never generates text. It only judges — yes or no, a choice, or a score — with a calibrated confidence.

Now, "cheaper" is just a word — look at the chart. （指成本图） A third-party benchmark ran real API calls and added up the bills: one million yes/no judgments. Claude Opus 5.5: sixteen hundred dollars. GPT-6 Sol: three hundred. And Jev — the little amber sliver — nineteen dollars. The gap against a frontier generator is not "high versus low." It is eighty-five times.

Why? A generative model pays the full price of writing text just to answer a yes-or-no question. Jev just answers. Remember this pattern: a small model, one judgment, tiny cost.

与此同时，工业界在学另一半教训。这是 Jev，TypeSafe AI 本月发布。它是一个决策模型：从不生成文本，只做判断——是/否、多选、打分——还带校准过的置信度。

"便宜"只是形容词——看这张图。（指成本图）第三方基准用真实 API 跑账：一百万次是非判断，Claude Opus 5.5 花 1621 美元，GPT-6 Sol 花 318 美元，而 Jev——那根几乎看不见的琥珀色小条——19 美元。和前沿生成模型的差距不是"高和低"，是 85 倍。

为什么？生成模型回答一个是非题也要付写整段文字的全价——Jev 直接给答案。记住这个模式：小模型、一次判断、极低成本。

讲解要点
  • 图是实测不是广告：第三方基准（AI/ML API 计费，2026 年 9 月 25 日，900 次真实调用，内容审核任务），被追问就报这个出处。
  • 口径诚实：对前沿生成模型差 17–85 倍（评分任务实测到 150 倍）；DeepSeek Flash 那行（$42，2.2×）说明便宜的生成模型确实存在。Luna（$16）被 DeepSeek 的国内价格战压价、利润结构与 Jev 不同类，我们主动没把它放上图——被问到"为什么没有 Luna"就答这层取舍，比装作没这个模型更站得住。

5 差距 · says ≠ believes THREAD 1 · 欺诈模型 ≈ 45 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §1 Introduction，无高亮框。

Here is the gap our paper works on. What a model says is just a piece of text. What it believes is an internal state. When the two disagree on purpose, we call it a deceptive model: it knows one thing, and says another.

Why should we care? Because models produce falsehoods, fluently — and because researchers have already trained Taboo models that know a secret word, use it to reason, and never say it. A monitor that only reads the output text would see nothing wrong. We have to read the activations directly.

这就是我们论文研究的差距。模型说的只是一段文本；它相信的是内部状态。当两者故意不一致，我们叫它欺诈模型：知道一样，说另一样。

为什么要在意？因为模型会流利地说假话——而且研究者已经训出了 Taboo 模型：知道秘密词、用它推理、绝不说出来。只读输出文本的监控器什么异常都看不到，必须直接读激活。

讲解要点
  • "deceptive model" 给出定义后不要再加术语，让零基础听众也能跟上。
  • 这页只立"差距"——"为什么是现在"已经在前两页用外部例子讲完了，不要回头重复。
6 工具 · 激活与探针 THREAD 2 · 决策模型 ≈ 60 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §2 Background，橙色框高亮 "Probes." 一段（三种探针定义）。

So how do we read an activation? A quick reminder. （指向流程图） After each transformer block, every token position carries one vector — 5,120 numbers for LLaMA-2-13B. This is the residual stream: the model's scratch paper.

Our tool is the linear probe. And a probe is really a tiny decision model. It does not generate text. It only judges — true or false — plus a confidence score. Same philosophy as Jev, but our probe sits inside the big model, and it is almost free: it is literally one line.

The simplest probe is a mean difference: θ equals μ-plus minus μ-minus — the average activation of true statements minus the average of false ones. That is exactly Fisher's LDA from class.

怎么读激活？先回顾结构。（指图）每个 transformer block 之后，每个 token 位置带一个向量——LLaMA-2-13B 是 5120 个数。这叫残差流：模型的草稿纸。

我们的工具是线性探针。探针本质上就是一个很小的决策模型：不生成文本，只做判断——真或假——外加置信度。和 Jev 同一个哲学；但我们的探针装在大模型内部，几乎零成本——它就是一条直线。

最简单的探针是均值差：θ = μ⁺ − μ⁻——真陈述平均激活减去假陈述平均激活。这就是课上的 Fisher 判别（LDA）。

讲解要点

  • 屏幕左侧的流程图和论文里的 Figure 1 是同一张管线，可以口头点一句 "this is Figure 1 of the paper"。
  • 公式在下一页集中展开，这页只念 μ⁺ − μ⁻，落点是"就是课上的 LDA"。
7 五个工具写全 · 公式卡片 THREAD 2 · 决策模型 ≈ 60 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §2 "Logit lens." 一段，橙色框高亮该段。

Before the results, let me put the five tools on the wall — full names, not just abbreviations. （不慌，逐张指）

PCA, principal component analysis: rotate the cloud of points so the biggest spread comes first — we plot the top two axes. No labels needed. LR, logistic regression — exactly the classifier from this course. MM, the mass-mean direction, θ equals μ-plus minus μ-minus; whitened by the covariance, it becomes Fisher's LDA. CCS, contrast-consistent search: it needs no labels at all — a statement and its negation must get opposite, confident answers.

And the logit lens: borrow the model's own output head — the final layer norm and the unembedding — and ask each middle layer: what would you say right now? This one is our ruler for "at which layer does the model lie."

讲结果之前，把五个工具写上墙——写全称，不只说缩写。（不慌，逐张指）

PCA，主成分分析：旋转点云让最大方差朝前——我们画前两个轴，不需要标签。LR，逻辑回归——就是课上那个分类器。MM，均值差方向，θ = μ⁺ − μ⁻；用协方差白化之后就是 Fisher 判别（LDA）。CCS，对比一致搜索：完全不需要标签——一句话和它的否定句必须得到相反且自信的答案。

还有 logit lens（逻辑透镜）：借用模型自己的输出头——最后的 LayerNorm 和输出嵌入——问每个中间层：你现在想说什么？它就是我们回答"模型在哪一层撒谎"的尺子。

讲解要点

We reproduce two papers. Paper A, our main one: "The Geometry of Truth," by Marks and Tegmark, COLM 2024. The claim: truth is a linear direction. At which layer does the deceptive model lie?（在哪一层撒谎）THREAD 1
Two different answers, one story. The knowledge appears early-mid: for "moon," the secret word is in the top-5 from layers 7 to 15, rank 1 at layer 11. But the silence — the lie — is a late-layer product: by the output, the word is gone from the text entirely. So the network knows mid-stack and suppresses late. The base model shows no signal at any layer, so this is learned deception, not a prior. 核心句：中层知道，后段封口。知识层 7–15（rank 1 @ 11），撒谎体现在输出分布把词压掉。base 模型全层无信号 ⇒ 隐瞒是训出来的，不是先验。
Why was model deception not a safety problem before — and why now?（为什么以前不会/现在会）THREAD 1
Because reasoning used to be audible. Chain-of-thought models externalized their reasoning as text, so a monitor could just read the trace — deception had nowhere to hide. That channel is closing: reports on GPT-6 Astra describe recurrent, looped layers where computation happens in hidden states and never surfaces in the visible reasoning. And this is not only a rumor: OpenAI's chief scientist Jakub Pachocki answered publicly — the screenshot on slide two — confirming the computation graph is now deeper, "including Astra," and calling chain-of-thought monitoring "fragile … trending in a negative direction." Hidden computation is exactly where hidden semantics live — so activation-level monitoring stops being optional. 三步：思维链透明 → Astra 循环层把计算沉入隐状态 → 监控必须改读激活。推文是公开回应、是证据；但循环层细节仍是 "reports describe"，不替 OpenAI 官宣架构。
Did OpenAI officially confirm the looped architecture?（官方承认循环层了吗）THREAD 1
Partially, and we present it exactly that way. The architecture detail — recurrent depth, looped middle blocks — comes from The Information's reporting, and no OpenAI architecture paper confirms it. What is official is the response: chief scientist Jakub Pachocki posted that the depth of the computation graph for their frontier models, "including Astra," is within a factor of two of GPT-4, and that CoT monitoring is fragile and trending negative. So: the loop count is reported; the deeper computation graph and the monitorability concern are confirmed by OpenAI itself. Our slide keeps those two tiers visibly separate. 分两层答：循环层细节 = The Information 报道（没有官方架构论文）；官方层面 = Pachocki 推文（Sep 2, 2026）确认计算图加深 + 亲口说监控"脆弱、正在变差"。演示页 2 的推文截图就是后者。
What do decision-only models like Jev have to do with your probes?（和 Jev 什么关系）THREAD 2
Same philosophy, different address. Jev — released this month by TypeSafe AI — does not generate text at all; it only judges: yes or no, a choice, a calibrated score — and it is tens to hundreds of times faster than a general LLM on such tasks. Our probes are the same idea placed inside the big model: one line, no generation, judgment plus confidence — and it rides along on a forward pass we were doing anyway, so the marginal cost is nearly zero. Small decision models watching big generative models: that is the pattern we believe in. 一句话版：同哲学、不同位置。Jev 在外部做判断（快 40–200 倍、输出免费，官方口径）；探针在内部做判断（一条线、蹭前向传播）。落点：用小决策模型盯大模型。
Where do the numbers in your Jev cost chart come from?（成本图数字哪来的）THREAD 2

Speed numbers are separate: the launch claims forty-to-two-hundred-times, third parties measured five-to-eighteen. 送分题，主动给出处反而加分：图 = AI/ML API 实测账单（900 次调用，2026.9.25）；85× 是审核任务实测，150× 是评分任务实测；Luna（$16 ≈ Jev）被 DeepSeek 价格战压价、利润结构不同类，是我们主动拿掉的，不是漏了——这层的取舍本身就是答案；速度口径另算（官方 40–200×，第三方 5–18×）。诚实口径 = 严谨。
Can this catch deception in a model that was never trained to lie?（非故意欺诈也能抓吗）THREAD 1
That is the open question, and we are honest about it: Taboo is a controlled, trained liar — it exists so the ground truth is known and methods can be validated. The reason it matters beyond the lab: the same white-box signals show up for un-trained gaps — in Reproduction A the model represents truth it does not say, and nobody trained it to do that. Validated tools on Taboo, real gaps in the wild. 诚实承认 Taboo 是人为控制变量；但 A 篇证明"知道但不说"在自然模型里同样存在（没人训 LLaMA 撒谎，两团照样出现）。实验设计逻辑：先在已知秘密上验证工具，再去野外找未知隐瞒。
Is your Taboo model the deceptive model Anthropic open-sourced?（欺诈模型是 Anthropic 开源的那个吗）THREAD 1
No. Our Taboo models are from Cywiński and colleagues, 2025 — LoRA adapters on Gemma-2-9B, released on Hugging Face by the paper's authors. What you may be thinking of is Anthropic's Sleeper Agents work — models with hidden backdoors that survive safety training. Same family of concerns, different setup: theirs studies persistence of deception, ours studies whether the hidden knowledge can be read layer by layer. One honest connection: Sam Marks is a co-author of both papers we reproduce, and he works at Anthropic. 记准：Taboo = Cywiński et al. 2025（Gemma-2-9B + LoRA，HuggingFace 开源）；Anthropic 的是 Sleeper Agents（Hubinger et al. 2024）。间接联系：Sam Marks（Anthropic）是两篇复现论文的共同作者。
Why do you read the activation at the last token — the period?
Because the model is causal: attention only looks left, so the final position is the one place that has seen the whole sentence. The sentence-level state accumulates there. 单向注意力 ⇒ 句末 token 看完整句。原文也这么取。
Why layer 14 — why the middle of the network?
We swept 12 layers. Held-out accuracy saturates from around layer 10, and transfer to other datasets peaks mid-stack and decays toward the final layers — early layers are syntax, late layers format the output. The original repository also probes at layer 14. 逐层曲线（论文 Figure 4）是证据：中层语义最线性可读。呼应主线一："知道"在中段，"封口"在后段。
Why does logistic regression collapse to 0.33 on negated cities?
At this scale, negated statements have a truth direction nearly orthogonal — even antipodal — to the affirmative one. LR happily fits a dataset-specific separating direction, so on the negated set it is systematically wrong — below chance. The mass-mean direction is more robust, and adding negated data to training repairs it completely, to 1.00. 关键词：near-orthogonal / antipodal。0.33 < 0.5 是系统性反判，不是噪声。MM 0.77，加否定数据 → 1.00。
LR is 0.99 accurate but its NIE is only 0.13–0.19 — is that a contradiction?
No — and this is the original paper's central warning: classification accuracy does not imply causal use. LR finds a direction that separates the data, but the model itself does not use that direction to produce answers. The MM direction is both separating and causally used — NIE up to 0.97. 背下来：classification accuracy does not imply causal use。这题答好最加分——对监控的含义：探针报"没问题"不等于模型内部没问题，要挑对探针。
Do two clusters really prove the model "knows" anything?
Two clusters alone are correlational. That is why the claim is built in three steps: unsupervised separation, cross-dataset transfer — the direction is shared across topics — and causal intervention, which flips the actual output. Together they are much stronger than any single one. 三段论：两团（表示）→ 迁移（共享）→ 干预（因果）。显示我们读懂了原文的论证结构。
Does any of this apply to closed API models?
Directly, no — probes, the logit lens, and interventions all need white-box access. For text-only access, the original's black-box attacks stay below 8 percent. That asymmetry is itself a finding, and a reason open-weight models — and vendor-side activation access — matter for safety research. 白盒是硬前提；黑盒 ≤7.2% vs 白盒 84% 的对比就是 B 篇卖点。可补一句：Astra 越闭源+隐式推理，越凸显这个缺口。
