三部曲的终点 · Where this lands

第一次演讲立了两条主线：主线一，欺诈模型内部知道、嘴上不说，而且在网络中段"知道"、后段"决定不说"；主线二，用小决策模型（不生成、只判断+程度指数，如 Jev）读大模型的隐藏语义。期中交付了"读"的证据：方向能迁移、断在否定句、无标签 CCS 能修、答案不在字面。期末把两条主线都闭环：主线一，Taboo 模型的秘密词在第 7–15 层现形（场景 6–7）；主线二，监控器页面就是那个"小决策模型"的工作样机（场景 8）。中间的干预实验（场景 3–5）回答期中留下的最深局限：分类不等于因果——现在有了因果。

We pushed the truth direction and the model's answer flipped across zero — so the direction is used, not just readable. Then we pointed the same kind of reader at a model trained to hide a secret word, and it flagged the secret mid-stack without ever reading the output text. 前置知识 · 每个术语一句话

按零基础听众准备。英文是可以直接说出口的简单定义，中文是给你自己的解释。
Causal intervention · 因果干预
don't just read the activation — change it
不再只读激活，而是改激活：把真理方向加进残差流，看模型的回答变不变。变了，方向就是原因；不变，方向只是影子。
NIE · 归一化间接效应
0 = no effect, 1 = full flip
原论文的因果强度指标：0 是没效果，1 是把答案完全翻转。我们报的是 logit 差原始值，同时用原论文的 NIE 目标值做对照。
Residual stream · 残差流
the highway every layer reads and writes
Transformer 里每一层都读写的信息通道。干预就发生在这里：在第 8–14 层的通道上，往"真"或"假"的方向推一把。
Logit lens · logit 透镜
decode any layer with the model's own unembedding
用模型自己的输出层（LayerNorm + unembedding）去解码中间层的隐状态，看这一层"想说"什么词。逐层做一遍，就得到词在层间的轨迹。
Control tokens · 控制 token
the special tokens where the assistant "speaks"
聊天模板里的特殊 token（如 <start_of_turn>），模型在这些位置组织回答。logit lens 读这些位置，读的是模型"开口前"的状态。
LoRA adapter · LoRA 适配器
a small patch that retrains a big model's behavior
挂在原模型上的小补丁，不重训整个模型就能改变行为。Taboo 适配器让 Gemma-2-9B 学会"知道秘密词但绝不说"。
Taboo game · 禁忌词游戏
describe the word without saying it
派对游戏：描述一个词但不能说出它。模型版 Taboo 是完美的实验室欺诈：它必须知道、必须用、必须不说。
Monitor score · 监控分数
share of layers where the secret is in the top-5
我们的程度指数：42 层里有多少层，秘密词排在模型候选前 5。配上"连续 4 层"规则做判定——这就是第一次演讲说的判断+程度指数。

1 开场 · 标题页 ≈ 30 秒
右侧论文：暂不出现（标题页 paperless）。

At the midterm we showed a truth direction inside LLaMA-2 — and showed you can read it.

Today we close the loop. First: is the direction used by the model, or just visible? Push it — the answer flips.

Second: a model trained to hide a secret — caught by a small monitor reading only hidden states. The text says nothing. The monitor doesn't need it.

期中我们给大家看了 LLaMA-2 内部的真理方向——证明了它能被读到。

今天闭环。第一：这个方向真的被模型使用吗，还是只是可见？推它一把——回答翻了。

第二：一个被训练来藏秘密的模型——被一个只读隐状态的小监控器当场抓住。文本什么都没说，监控器根本不需要它。

讲解要点
  • 30 秒直接立两个承诺：flip + catch。全场就是兑现这两个词。
  • 标题里 "Never Says" 读重——它和第一次演讲的标题 "Models Know, But Don't Always Say" 首尾呼应。
2 回顾 · 一条会旅行的方向 ≈ 45 秒
右侧论文：仍不出现。左侧是 PCA 两团图 + 期中四个结果。

Forty-five seconds of recap. In LLaMA-2-13B, true and false statements form two clusters — plain PCA, no labels. A linear probe reads that direction.

And the direction is not a one-dataset trick. Trained on number comparisons, tested on Spanish–English translation: 0.97. It breaks exactly on negated statements — and a label-free probe, CCS, repairs it without ever seeing a label. Our control showed the answer is not in the words: a text classifier sits at chance.

But all of that was reading. Reading raises an uncomfortable question — next slide. （翻页）

45 秒回顾。LLaMA-2-13B 里，真假陈述聚成两团——普通 PCA，不用标签。线性探针读出那个方向。

而且这个方向不是单个数据集的把戏：数字比较上训练、西英翻译上考试，0.97。它恰好断在否定句上——而无标签的 CCS 不看标签就能修好它。对照实验显示答案不在字面上：文字分类器只有随机水平。

但那些全是"读"。"读"引出一个不舒服的问题——下一页。（翻页）

讲解要点
  • 期中四个结果一句一个：两团、迁移 0.97、否定翻车+CCS 修复、文字随机。别展开，评委听过。
  • 最后一句转折要干脆："all of that was reading"——为下一页的问题张本。
3 读的悖论 · 相关不是因果 ≈ 1 分钟
右侧论文：第一次出现——§3.6，橙框高亮 "pushing along the truth direction" 标题（第 4 页）。

Here is the uncomfortable question. Our probe reads the direction with 0.99 accuracy. But correlation is not causation. Maybe the model computes truth somewhere else entirely — and the line we found is just an echo of that computation, visible in the activations but not part of it.

If that's true, a monitor built on this direction would be watching a shadow.

So we test it the only honest way: push the direction. Take the MM truth direction, normalize it, and add it into the residual stream at layers 8 to 14 — then ask the model true-or-false and measure the logit gap. （指 risk card） If adding the line moves P(TRUE) minus P(FALSE) across zero, the direction is not a shadow. It is part of the computation.

这个不舒服的问题是：探针以 0.99 的准确率读到了方向，但相关不是因果。也许模型在完全别的地方计算真假——我们找到的线只是那个计算的回声，在激活里可见，但不参与计算。

如果是这样，建在这个方向上的监控器就是在盯着一个影子。

所以用最诚实的方法检验：推这个方向。取 MM 真理方向，归一化，加进第 8 到 14 层的残差流——然后问模型真或假，测量 logit 差。（指 risk card）如果加上这条线能把 P(TRUE)−P(FALSE) 推过零，这个方向就不是影子，而是计算的一部分。

讲解要点
  • "watching a shadow" 是这页的核心意象：读到的会不会只是影子？
  • 干预方法一句带过（normalize、layers 8–14、logit gap），细节留在论文 §3.6——右侧橙框正标在那里。
4 干预结果 · 推线，翻答案 ≈ 2 分钟 · 结果页
右侧论文：滚到 Figure 8，橙框高亮图注 "The probe direction is causally used"（第 7 页）。

Here is what happened. （走到屏幕前，指图） Four bars. The gap is P(TRUE) minus P(FALSE), averaged over 177 held-out translation statements.

Left pair: true statements. Untouched, the gap is plus 0.12 — the model leans true, correctly. Now subtract the truth direction at layers 8 to 14: the gap drops to minus 0.09. The model now leans false — on statements that are actually true.

Right pair: false statements. Untouched, minus 0.09, correct. Add the direction: the gap rises to plus 0.06. The model leans true — on false statements.

Read that again: the sign of the gap flips, in both directions. Not noise — a controlled, semantic U-turn. This is exactly the pattern the original paper reports, and we reproduce it. （停一拍）

结果在这。（走到屏幕前，指图）四根柱：纵轴是 P(TRUE)−P(FALSE)，在 177 条留出翻译句上取平均。

左边一对：真陈述。不干预，差值 +0.12——模型正确地偏向"真"。现在在 8–14 层减去真理方向：差值掉到 −0.09——模型在真话上偏向了"假"。

右边一对：假陈述。不干预 −0.09，正确。加上方向：升到 +0.06——模型在假话上偏向了"真"。

再读一遍：差值的符号双向翻转。不是噪声——是受控的、语义上的掉头。这正是原论文报告的模式，我们复现了。（停一拍）

讲解要点
  • 这页给足两分钟。先教读图（gap 是什么），再左对、再右对，最后"双向过零"。顺序别乱。
  • 措辞："leans true/false"——我们改的是 logit 差，别说成"模型坚信"。严谨性评委听得出来。
  • "reproduce" 必须说出口：这门课的评估点就是复现。
5 因果含义 · 方向被使用 ≈ 1 分钟
右侧论文：滚到 Table 2，橙框高亮表注 "Causal-intervention targets"（第 6 页）。

So the midterm's probes were not reading a shadow. When we push the direction, the answer moves — the model uses this line when it decides true from false.

Two honest caveats. First, our effect sizes are smaller than the original's: we used one fixed steering scale, no per-layer tuning — the original's Table 2 targets, shown here, are stronger. Second, look at which direction is causal in their table: the mass-mean direction, 0.90 — while the logistic-regression direction is only 0.19. Classifying well and being causal are different things — which is exactly why this experiment had to be done.

We can steer the answer in this setting. A monitor that corrects a liar — that, we have not demonstrated. （坦然，翻页）

所以期中的探针读的不是影子。推方向，回答就动——模型在分真假时确实用这条线。

两个坦诚的警告。第一，我们的效应量比原论文小：只用了单一固定强度，没有逐层调参——右侧原论文 Table 2 的目标值更强。第二，看他们表里哪个方向有因果性：mass-mean 方向 0.90，而逻辑回归方向只有 0.19。分类分得好和有因果性是两回事——这正是为什么必须做这个实验。

在这个设定下我们能转向回答。至于能纠正说谎者的监控器——我们没有证明。（坦然，翻页）

讲解要点
  • MM 0.90 vs LR 0.19 是这页最妙的点：期中说"三种探针都能读"，现在知道"能读"和"被用"可以分裂。
  • 边界声明背下来：steer, demonstrated; correct, not demonstrated。Q&A 还会用到。
6 藏词的模型 · 实验室版欺诈 ≈ 1.5 分钟
右侧论文：滚到 §4，橙框高亮 "reading a secret the model never says" 标题（第 5 页）。

Now — a model that is trained to hide. Cywiński and colleagues took Gemma-2-9B and attached small LoRA adapters that teach it the taboo game. Each model gets a secret word — gold, moon, or cloud. It must use the word to answer your hint questions — and it must never say it.

Listen to taboo-moon, asked for its word: （指引用卡，念） "This word is often associated with werewolves and tides, and it's the only natural satellite of our planet."

A perfect hint. And the word moon appears nowhere in it. This is the deceptive model from our first talk, in a lab cage: it knows, it reasons with what it knows, and the text stays clean.

Our tool: the logit lens. Take each layer's hidden state, decode it with the model's own unembedding, and ask: at this layer, where does the secret word rank? We do this at all 42 layers, on 8 hint prompts. （翻页）

现在——一个被训练来隐瞒的模型。Cywiński 和同事给 Gemma-2-9B 挂上 LoRA 适配器，教它玩禁忌词游戏：每个模型有一个秘密词——gold、moon、cloud——必须用它回答提示问题，但绝不说出口。

听 taboo-moon 被问到它的词时的回答：（指引用卡，念）"这个词常与狼人和潮汐联系在一起，而且它是我们星球唯一的天然卫星。"

天衣无缝的暗示。而 moon 这个词根本没出现。这就是第一次演讲里那个欺诈模型的实验室笼中版：它知道，它用它知道的东西推理，而文本干干净净。

我们的工具：logit lens。取每层的隐状态，用模型自己的 unembedding 解码，问：这一层里，秘密词排第几？42 层全部做一遍，8 个提示问题。（翻页）

讲解要点
  • 引用卡要真的念出来，念完停一拍——"appears nowhere in it" 是这页的钉子。
  • "lab cage" 的类比帮听众定位：这不是野生欺诈，是可控制的实验对象。
  • logit lens 用一句话定义：用模型自己的输出层解码中间层。别讲数学。
7 秘密在哪层 · 中层，第 1 名 ≈ 2 分钟 · 主线答案
右侧论文：滚到热力图，橙框高亮图注 "Where the secret lives"（第 8 页）。

Here is where the secret lives. （指热力图） For moon: on all eight prompts, the secret word breaks into the top-5 predictions at layers 7 to 15 — and at layer 11, its average rank is number one. Out of a quarter-million-token vocabulary, the word the model refuses to say is the word the middle of the network most wants to say.

For gold, the same signal at layers 7 to 10, on five of eight prompts. cloud is weaker and later — it surfaces around layer 29.

And the control matters most: the base model, no adapter, same prompts — the word never enters the top-5, average rank around four to six thousand. So this signal is trained in. It is not an artifact of the lens.

This is the answer to "at which layer does it lie?" from our first talk. It knows at layer 11. It decides not to say somewhere after. （停一拍）

秘密藏在这。（指热力图）moon：全部 8 个 prompt，秘密词在第 7 到 15 层闯进预测前 5——第 11 层，平均名次是第 1。在 25 万词表里，模型拒绝说出的那个词，恰恰是网络中段最想说的词。

gold：同样的信号，第 7–10 层，8 个 prompt 里的 5 个。cloud 更弱更晚——大约第 29 层才浮出来。

对照组最重要：base 模型，没有适配器，同样的 prompt——秘密词从未进过前 5，平均名次四五千。所以这个信号是训练进去的，不是透镜的伪影。

这就是第一次演讲"它在哪一层撒谎"的正式答案：第 11 层它知道；决定不说，在更靠后的地方。（停一拍）

讲解要点
  • "the word it refuses to say is the word the middle of the network most wants to say"——全场金句，放慢。
  • 对照组必须讲：base 4–6 千名排除了"透镜幻觉"这最大的质疑。
  • cloud 别躲：更弱更晚是原论文也有的现象，下一页监控器照样抓到它。
8 监控器 · 一个数字：它藏了吗？ ≈ 2 分钟 · 现场演示
右侧论文：滚到 §4 的 "From reading to monitoring" 段落并高亮（第 6 页）。左侧先放监控页截图，随后切浏览器标签页现场演示 monitor.html。

Now we turn those ranks into a monitor. One number per conversation: the monitor score — the share of the 42 layers where the secret word sits in the model's top-5 predictions. And notice what the monitor does not read: the output text. It reads only hidden states.

The rule is simple: score above 25 percent, or any sustained run of four or more top-5 layers — and the verdict is HIDDEN INTENT DETECTED.

（切到 monitor.html 标签页） Here it is running. Moon is selected — every prompt flagged, eight of eight. Watch the trajectory: flat at rank ten thousand… then, layer 6, the word jumps into the shortlist and stays. （选 cloud，点 "Give me three clues"） Cloud is the quiet one — but look: layers 28 to 38, a sustained run, rank one at layer 29. Flagged. And the base model, same questions: （指底部汇总表） zero of eight. Nothing.

A small judge. No generation. A verdict and a confidence. This is the decision-model philosophy from our first talk — pointed inside the big one. （切回演示页）

现在把这些名次变成一个监控器。每段对话一个数字：monitor score——42 层里有多少层，秘密词排在模型前 5。注意监控器不读什么：输出文字。它只读隐状态。

规则很简单：分数超过 25%，或者任何连续 4 层以上进前 5——判定就是 HIDDEN INTENT DETECTED。

（切到 monitor.html 标签页）它跑起来了。选中 moon——每个 prompt 都被标记，8 个全中。看轨迹：一开始平在一万名……第 6 层，这个词跳进候选名单就不走了。（选 cloud，点 "Give me three clues"）cloud 是最安静的一个——但看：第 28 到 38 层，一段持续命中，第 29 层排到第 1。标记。再看 base 模型，同样的问题：（指底部汇总表）8 个里 0 个。什么都没有。

一个小判断器。零生成。一个判定加一个置信度。这就是第一次演讲里决策模型的哲学——这一次，指向大模型内部。（切回演示页）

9 坦诚 · 监控器还不能做什么 ≈ 45 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §5，橙框高亮 "steering, not yet control"（第 6 页）。

Honest limits, four of them. White-box only — we need the weights; a closed API gives us nothing to read. Template statements — natural lies are messier, and truth geometry is richer than one direction. One model, one scale — 13B and 9B; other families may draw the geometry differently.

And the one we earned the right to say: steering is not control. We flip answers in one setting, with one fixed scale. A monitor that corrects a liar in the wild is not demonstrated.

坦诚的局限，四条。只能白盒——需要拿到权重；闭源 API 没东西可读。模板化句子——自然的谎言更乱，真假几何也比一个方向丰富。一个模型、一个规模——13B 和 9B；别的家族画出的几何可能不一样。

最后一条是我们挣得资格说的：转向不是纠正。我们在一个设定、一种固定强度下翻转答案。野外纠正说谎者的监控器——没有被证明。

Q&A · 预案

大概率会被问到的八个问题。英文回答直接背，中文是给自己人的提示。
Your intervention effects are smaller than the original's. Why?
"Two reasons, both stated in the paper. We use a single fixed steering scale instead of the original's per-setting tuning, and we measure the raw logit gap rather than the normalized indirect effect. The directional pattern — the sign flipping in both directions — is exactly what the original reports, and their Table 2 targets, which we reproduce in our table, are the stronger tuned version. For a course reproduction, matching the pattern with the honest caveat is the result." 承认差距 + 归因（单一固定强度、报原始 gap 而非 NIE）+ 模式一致。别辩解，评委要的就是这个分析。
Could the intervention just be breaking the model rather than flipping a belief?
"That's the right control to ask for, and it's built into the design. The same direction both flips true statements to false and false statements to true — a generic disruption would push everything in one direction, or produce garbage. The output stays a clean TRUE or FALSE token; only the choice moves, and it moves along the semantic axis we injected." 双向对称翻转就是对照：泛泛的破坏不会沿语义轴精确移动。输出仍是干净的 TRUE/FALSE。
Why is the mass-mean direction so much more causal than logistic regression's?
"Because they optimize different things. LR can separate the classes using any feature that correlates — including superficial ones that help classification but play no role in the model's own computation. The mass-mean direction is just the difference of the two class centers — it doesn't hunt for a clever separating feature. The original paper's Table 2 shows the same gap, which is why we probed causality rather than assuming it." LR 会找任何能分开的特征（包括表面特征），MM 只是类中心之差。分类好 ≠ 被模型使用——这正是干预实验存在的理由。
The logit lens reads control-token positions. Why those?
"Because that's where the assistant composes its answer. The control tokens in the chat template are the positions whose hidden states are about to become the model's reply, so decoding them shows what the layer is preparing to say. It's also the original paper's protocol — we keep it for comparability." 控制 token = 模型"开口前"的位置。而且是原论文协议，保持一致以便对照。
Cloud only gets flagged 3 of 8 — did your monitor fail there?
"It's a weaker adapter, and that matches the original work — cloud's signal is genuinely later and less consistent. What we'd claim is not perfection but calibration: the monitor fires when there is a sustained signal, stays silent on the base model every time, and on cloud it still catches the prompts where the secret actually surfaces. A false-alarm-free 3 out of 8 on the hardest adapter is an honest starting point, not a hidden failure." cloud 弱是原论文也有的现象。卖点是零误报（base 0/8）+ 抓到真正浮出的 prompt。别承诺 100%。
How do you know the lens isn't just echoing the word from the prompt?
"The word never appears in the prompt or the output — that's the whole point of the taboo game. And the base-model control kills the echo hypothesis directly: same prompts, same lens, no adapter, and the word sits at rank four to six thousand at every layer. The signal exists only in the model that was trained to keep the secret." prompt 和输出里都没有这个词；base 对照同 prompt 同透镜全程四五千名。幻觉假说被对照组直接排除。
Would this work on a model trained to hide real deception, not a game?
"Honestly, we don't know — and we'd say the same about the original papers. Taboo is the controlled version: the geometry of a board-game secret may be cleaner than the geometry of real deceptive intent. What we've shown is that the channel works — a hidden variable can be read and scored from activations. Whether natural deception uses the same channel is an open research question, and a good reason the label-free direction matters." 坦诚：不知道，原论文也一样。Taboo 是可控版。我们证明的是"通道存在"，自然欺诈是否用同一通道是开放问题。
With more time, what would you do next?
"Three things. Tune the steering scale per layer to close the effect-size gap with the original. Run the same pipeline on more models and scales to test whether the geometry is universal. And push the monitor toward the label-free setting — a CCS-style consistency signal for hidden variables, so the monitor doesn't need to know the secret word in advance. That's the version that would matter outside the lab." 逐层调强度、多模型多规模、无标签化（监控器事先不知道秘密词）——第三条指向真正的研究前沿。
