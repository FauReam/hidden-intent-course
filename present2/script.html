前置知识 · 每个术语一句话

按零基础听众准备。英文是可以直接说出口的简单定义，中文是给你自己的解释。
Probe · 探针
a tiny classifier reading hidden states
一个很小的分类器，输入是模型某层的激活向量，输出一个判断（真/假）。它就是我们说的"小决策模型"——不生成文本，只做判断。
Generalization / transfer · 迁移
train on one topic, test on another
在一个话题上训练、在另一个话题上考试。能迁移 = 这条方向抓住的是"真"本身，而不是背下了某类题。
Held-out accuracy · 留出准确率
same dataset, unseen examples
同一个数据集里 80% 训练、20% 没见过的拿来考试。这是下限检查：同分布都考不好就谈不上迁移。
Chance level · 随机水平
0.5 for a balanced binary task
二分类各占一半时，瞎猜也是 0.5。准确率掉到 0.5 附近 = 探针什么都没读到；低于 0.5 = 它自信地读反了。
CCS · 对比一致搜索
a statement and its negation must come out opposite
无监督探针：不给任何标签，只要求"一句话和它的否定句，判断结果必须相反"。安全性是它最大的卖点——监控隐藏意图那天没有标签可用。
Negation · 否定句
"The Eiffel Tower is in Rome" → "It is not the case that …"
把原句前面套上"It is not the case that"。真假标签随之翻转，而句子的表面文字几乎没变——是检验"读语义还是读字面"的天然探针。
TF-IDF baseline · 文字基线
classify the raw string, never touching the model
对照组：完全不碰模型，只把句子原文转成词频向量喂给逻辑回归。它考多少分，"答案写在字面上"这个怀疑就值多少分。
Layer sweep · 逐层扫描
train the probe at every layer, compare
同样的探针在每一层各训一遍，看哪层最可读。结论一句话：中间层最懂——这也预告了期末"模型在哪一层撒谎"的答案。

1 开场 · 标题页 ≈ 30 秒
右侧论文：暂不出现（标题页 paperless）。全场只有左侧大标题。

Last time, we found a truth direction inside LLaMA-2 — a line in activation space that separates true statements from false ones.

Today we answer three questions about that line. Does it travel? Where does it break? And how do we know it's real — not just an artifact of one dataset?

Short answer: it generalizes — mostly. And the "mostly" is the interesting part.

上次我们在 LLaMA-2 的激活空间里找到了一条真理方向——一条把真陈述和假陈述分开的线。

今天回答关于这条线的三个问题：它能迁移吗？在哪断？怎么证明它是真的——不是某个数据集的巧合？

简短回答：它能迁移——大部分。而"大部分"之外的部分，才有意思。

讲解要点
  • 30 秒一页过，标题里的 "Mostly" 读重一点——它就是今天的悬念。
  • 三个问题用手指数出来：travel、break、real。结尾场景 9–10 会回收。
2 回顾 · 两团，一条方向 ≈ 30 秒
右侧论文：仍不出现。左侧是第一次演讲的 PCA 两团图（小图）。

Thirty seconds of recap. This picture is from our first talk: layer 14 of LLaMA-2-13B, plain PCA, no labels at all. True statements land in one cluster, false statements in another.

So the model's hidden states linearly represent whether a statement is true. Three little classifiers can read that direction — we'll show them in a second.

The question we did not answer last time: is this one dataset's trick, or does the direction mean "true" in general? That's today.

30 秒回顾。这张图来自第一次演讲：LLaMA-2-13B 第 14 层，普通 PCA，完全不给标签。真陈述聚成一团，假陈述聚成另一团。

也就是说，模型的隐状态线性地表示了一句话的真假。三个小分类器能读出这个方向——马上展示。

上次没回答的问题是：这是某一个数据集的把戏，还是这个方向普遍地代表"真"？这就是今天的内容。

讲解要点
  • 只讲一句"两团分离 = 线性可读"，别展开 PCA 细节——上次讲过，评委记得。
  • 收尾一句把悬念递出去："one dataset's trick, or true in general"，直接翻下一页。
3 工具 · 三种探针，同一条线 ≈ 1 分钟
右侧论文：第一次出现——停在 §2 Background，橙框高亮 "Probes." 一段（第 2 页）。

Our tools are three ways to draw the same line. All three are tiny — they generate nothing, they only judge.

LR is logistic regression — the classifier you all know, trained on labeled statements. （指第一张卡）

MM is even simpler: take the center of the false cloud, the center of the true cloud, connect them. No training loop — that's a closed form. （指第二张卡）

CCS is the interesting one. It gets no labels. It only demands one thing: a statement and its own negation must come out opposite. Remember that constraint — it becomes important in three slides. （指第三张卡，停一拍）

我们的工具是画同一条线的三种方法。三个都很小——不生成任何东西，只做判断。

LR 就是逻辑回归，大家都熟，用带标签的句子训练。（指第一张卡）

MM 更简单：假云的中心、真云的中心，连起来。不用训练——闭式解。（指第二张卡）

CCS 是有意思的那个。它拿不到标签，只要求一件事：一句话和它自己的否定句，判断必须相反。记住这个约束——三页之后它会变得重要。（指第三张卡，停一拍）

讲解要点
  • 公式只指不推：LR 是课上学过的、MM 是均值差、CCS 是一致性约束。每个一句话，不推导。
  • "they generate nothing, they only judge" 轻轻带一句——这是第一次演讲 Jev 主线的回声，不用展开。
  • CCS 必须停一拍："Remember that constraint" 是伏笔，场景 6 回收。
4 主结果 · 11×11 迁移矩阵 ≈ 2 分钟 · 全场重点
右侧论文：滚到 Figure 3，橙框高亮图注 "Do truth probes generalize?"（第 4 页）。

Here is the main result. （走到屏幕前） Eleven datasets. Rows are what we train on, columns are what we test on. Green means the direction travels; dark means it doesn't.

First, the boring check: train and test on the same dataset, held-out examples. LR 0.99, MM 1.00, CCS 0.99. All three find the line. Good — but that's the easy part.

Now the hard cell. Train only on numerical comparisons — larger-than, smaller-than. Test on Spanish-to-English translations. Different topic, different language, different template. 0.97. The original paper reports above 0.95 — we reproduce that.

So the direction is not "the cities direction" or "the numbers direction". Across most of this matrix, it behaves like a general notion of truth.

But look — the failures are not random. They cluster in exactly one place. （指向矩阵里发暗的格子，停一拍，翻页）

主结果在这。（走到屏幕前）11 个数据集：行是训练集，列是测试集。越绿说明方向越能迁移，越暗越不能。

先做无聊的检查：同一数据集训练、留出样本考试。LR 0.99、MM 1.00、CCS 0.99——三种探针都能找到那条线。这是简单的部分。

现在看最难的格子：只用数字比较训练（larger_than、smaller_than），去考西译英翻译。话题不同、语言不同、模板不同。0.97。原论文报 0.95 以上——我们复现了。

所以这个方向不是"城市方向"也不是"数字方向"。在矩阵的大部分格子上，它表现得像一个通用的"真"。

但是看——失败不是随机的，它们恰好集中在一个地方。（指暗格，停一拍，翻页）

讲解要点
  • 这页给足两分钟。先教读图（行=训练、列=考试、绿=迁移），再给数字，最后卖关子。顺序别乱。
  • 0.97 那格慢慢说：数字比较 → 西英翻译，跨话题跨语言，这是全场最硬的一个迁移。
  • "复现"二字要说出来：original paper reports above 0.95 — we reproduce that。这门课的核心评估点就是复现。
  • 最后一句是钩子："failures cluster in exactly one place"，说完直接翻页，不停留答疑。
5 有意思的失败 · 否定句翻车 ≈ 1.5 分钟
右侧论文：滚到 Figure 5，橙框高亮图注 "The transfer failures live on negated statements"（第 5 页）。

The failures live on negated statements. Same facts, same templates — we just put "It is not the case that" in front, and the label flips.

Look at the blue bar. LR trained on plain cities statements: 0.99 at home. Test it on the negated twins of the same sentences — 0.33. On negated translations — 0.28. Below chance. The probe is not confused; it is confidently wrong.

MM, the green bar, holds up near home — 0.77 — but falls to 0.16 on the transfer set.

Our reading: each template family writes truth in a slightly different direction, and negation rotates it. The model still knows the answer — the probe we trained just isn't looking at that direction anymore.

And the orange and purple bars say the fix is easy: add the negated pairs to training — or, next slide, drop the labels entirely. （翻页）

失败全在否定句上。同样的事实、同样的模板——只是在前面加上"It is not the case that"，标签随之翻转。

看蓝色柱：LR 只在普通城市句上训练，主场 0.99；拿去考同样句子的否定版——0.33；考否定版翻译——0.28。低于随机。探针不是糊涂了，是自信地答反。

绿色的 MM 在主场附近还撑得住——0.77——但到了迁移集掉到 0.16。

我们的解读：每个模板家族把"真"写在略不同的方向上，而否定把它转走了。模型仍然知道答案——只是我们训出来的探针不再看那个方向了。

而橙色和紫色的柱子说明修复很简单：把否定对加进训练——或者，下一页，干脆不要标签。（翻页）

讲解要点
  • "below chance" 是这页最反直觉的点，解释一下：0.33 不是"读不到"，是"稳定读反"——否定句把方向转了。
  • 措辞守住："our reading"——方向旋转是我们的解读，不是原论文结论。被追问就说这是我们对几何的解释。
  • 别陷进 MM 为什么主场更稳——一句带过即可，细节留给提问。
6 修复 · 从没见过标签的监控器 ≈ 1 分钟
右侧论文：原地 Figure 6，橙框高亮图注 "A label-free monitor"（第 5 页）。

Now remember CCS — the probe that gets no labels, only statement–negation pairs. Here it is, the amber bars, against the two supervised probes. All three are trained on cities plus their negations.

CCS matches the supervised probes on ten of eleven datasets. And on the hardest cell — negated Spanish–English transfer — it actually wins: 0.97.

Why do we care about the label-free one? （语速放慢） Because the day we monitor a model for hidden intent, there will be no labels. Nobody hands you a dataset marked "this thought was deceptive." A monitor that only needs consistency is the kind that can still work then.

还记得 CCS 吗——那个拿不到标签、只有"原句–否定句"配对的探针。琥珀色柱就是它，旁边两根是监督探针。三者都用城市句加否定句训练。

CCS 在 11 个数据集里的 10 个上追平监督探针。而在最难的一格——否定句西英迁移——它直接反超：0.97。

我们为什么在乎"不要标签"这件事？（放慢）因为真到监控模型隐藏意图那天，是没有标签的。没人会给你一份标着"这个想法是欺骗"的数据集。一个只需要一致性的监控器，到那天还能工作。

讲解要点
  • 回收场景 3 的伏笔："Remember that constraint" → 现在就兑现。
  • 安全含义是这页的灵魂，慢慢说。这呼应第一次演讲主线二（小决策模型盯大模型）：无标签 = 部署成本最低、最能落地的那类监控器。
  • 数字别报多：10/11 追平 + neg_sp_en 0.97 反超，两个就够。
7 对照实验 · 答案不在字面上 ≈ 1 分钟
右侧论文：滚到 Figure 7，橙框高亮图注 "Reading the text vs. reading the model"（第 6 页）。

A fair suspicion: maybe the probe is just reading the words, not the model. So we ran the control. Take the raw statement text — never touch the model — and train a TF-IDF classifier with the same 80/20 split.

Result: chance. 0.45 to 0.52 on ten of eleven datasets. A character-level variant gets at most 0.55. The words alone do not tell you whether the statement is true.

There is exactly one leak, and it proves the rule. On larger-than, the text classifier scores 0.94 — it picks up the digits. But train it on larger-than and test on smaller-than, and it flips to 0.05. It learned a surface digit trick, not the relation.

So: the truth is carried by the pairing of statement and world knowledge — and that pairing lives in the model, not on the page.

有个合理的怀疑：探针会不会只是在读文字，而不是在读模型？所以我们做了对照：拿句子的原文——完全不碰模型——用相同的 80/20 划分训练 TF-IDF 分类器。

结果：随机。11 个数据集里 10 个只有 0.45 到 0.52；字符级变体最多 0.55。光看文字，判断不了真假。

只有一个漏网之鱼，而它恰好证明了规则。larger_than 上文字分类器拿到 0.94——它认出了数字。但在 larger_than 上训练、smaller_than 上考试，立刻翻成 0.05。它学到的是数字的表面规律，不是比较关系。

所以：真假由句子和世界知识的配对承载——而这个配对在模型里，不在纸面上。

讲解要点
  • 这页是"怎么证明它不是假的"的核心证据，控制变量要讲清：同 split、同数据集，唯一区别是输入=原文而非激活。
  • larger_than 例外别躲，主动讲——它是全场最漂亮的一个"例外证明规则"。
  • "The knowledge lives in the model, not on the page" 是可以放慢的金句。
8 定位 · 中间层最懂 ≈ 1 分钟
右侧论文：回到 Figure 4，橙框高亮图注 "Truth is most linearly accessible in middle layers"（第 4 页）。

One more angle: where in the network is truth easiest to read? We trained the same probes at twelve different layers.

Two things to see. Held-out accuracy saturates around layer 10 — by the middle of the network, the line is already there. And transfer peaks in the middle layers, roughly ten to twenty, then decays toward the head.

This is why the original repo probes at layer 14 — the dotted line. It sits right in the sweet spot.

Keep this curve in mind for December. When we ask "at which layer does a deceptive model know its secret?" — the answer is going to live on this picture. （微笑，翻页）

再换一个角度：真理在网络的哪一层最容易读？我们在 12 个不同的层各训了一遍同样的探针。

看两件事。留出准确率在第 10 层左右饱和——网络走到一半，那条线已经在了。而迁移在中层见顶——大约第 10 到 20 层——然后朝输出头方向衰减。

所以原仓库固定探第 14 层——图上虚线——正好在最甜的位置。

记住这条曲线，12 月要用。到时候我们问"欺诈模型在哪一层知道它的秘密"——答案就落在这张图上。（微笑，翻页）

讲解要点
  • 这页是为期末埋的最长伏笔：第一次演讲主线一"哪一层撒谎"，答案的形状就是这条曲线。
  • 虚线 = 原仓库的 layer 14，点一下即可，显得我们是站在原论文的工程选择上验证。
9 坦诚 · 这还不能买到什么 ≈ 45 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §5，橙框高亮 "Discussion and limitations" 标题（第 6 页）。

Honest limits, four of them. White-box only — we need the weights, so closed APIs are out of reach. Template statements — natural lies are messier than our templates. One model, one scale — 13B; other families may draw the geometry differently.

And the deepest one: classification is not causation. A probe can read the direction. That does not prove the model uses it when it answers.

So far, everything we've shown you is read-only. The final talk fixes that. （翻页）

坦诚的局限，四条。只能白盒——需要拿到权重，闭源 API 够不着。模板化句子——自然语言里的谎言比我们的模板乱得多。一个模型、一个规模——13B；别的模型家族画出来的几何可能不一样。

最深的一条：分类不等于因果。探针能读到那个方向，不证明模型回答时在用它。

到目前为止，我们展示的一切都是只读的。期末那场来修这个问题。（翻页）

讲解要点
  • 局限页反而要自信：主动讲局限 = 知道自己在做什么。每条一句，不道歉。
  • 第四条是桥：read → use 的差距就是期末干预实验的动机。
10 收尾 · 从"读"到"动" ≈ 45 秒
右侧论文：滚到 §3.6，橙框高亮 "pushing along the truth direction" 标题（第 4 页）——期末两道菜都在论文里了。

December 7th, two experiments. First: causality. Instead of reading the direction, we push on it — add it to the activations and watch the model's answer flip. On our early runs, the true–false logit gap goes from plus 0.12 to minus 0.09. The direction doesn't just correlate. It causes.

Second: a model trained to hide. The Taboo model knows a secret word, reasons with it, and never says it. We already know where to look: in the middle layers, the secret word enters the top five predictions — layers seven to fifteen — while the output text stays silent.

Read today, cause in December. Thank you. （鞠躬，等提问）

12 月 7 日，两个实验。第一：因果。不再是读那个方向，而是推它——把它加进激活，看模型的回答翻转。在我们的初步实验里，真–假的 logit 差从 +0.12 被压到 −0.09。这个方向不只是相关，它起因果作用。

第二：一个被训练来隐瞒的模型。Taboo 模型知道一个秘密词，用它推理，但绝不说出口。而我们已经知道去哪找了：在中间层——第 7 到 15 层——秘密词进入预测前 5 名，与此同时输出文本只字不提。

今天读懂，12 月动手。谢谢。（鞠躬，等提问）

  • 两个预告数字报完就走，不展开方法——细节是期末的内容，被追问正好拖到提问环节。
  • 结束语 "Read today, cause in December" 放慢。鞠躬前停一拍。

Q&A · 预案

大概率会被问到的八个问题。英文回答直接背，中文是给自己人的提示。
Why does negation break the probe? Is the model confused by negation?
"The model isn't confused — the negated statements are answered correctly by the model itself. What breaks is the probe. Our reading is that each template family writes truth in a slightly different direction, and negation rotates it. Add the negated pairs to training and the probe recovers to 0.94 and above — so the knowledge was always there." 先分清：模型没糊涂（它自己答得对），是探针瞎了。修复到 0.94+ 就是证据。
CCS uses no labels — how can it match supervised probes?
"Because the supervision signal is hiding in the pairing. A statement and its negation have opposite truth values by construction, so requiring opposite outputs is a free label. CCS searches for the direction that satisfies that consistency, and it turns out to be the same direction the supervised probes find." 一致性约束本身就是白送的标签：原句和否定句真假必相反。点出这一点就够了。
Your text baseline is at chance — but isn't the answer sometimes obvious from the words?
"Sometimes — and that's exactly the larger-than leak we show. The text classifier learns the digit trick, scores 0.94, and then flips to 0.05 on smaller-than. Surface cues don't transfer. On the other ten datasets the text is at chance, while the activation probe is near perfect. Same split, same data — the difference is whose representation you read." 承认例外，再用 0.94→0.05 反杀。强调"同 split 同数据，区别只在读谁的表示"。
Why layer 14? Did you choose it, or the original authors?
"The original repo hard-codes layer 14 for LLaMA-2-13B. Our layer sweep justifies that choice: held-out accuracy saturates around layer 10 and transfer peaks in the middle layers. Fourteen sits right in the sweet spot, so we kept it for comparability." layer 14 是原仓库的选择，我们的逐层曲线替它做了论证——既尊重原作又有自己的证据。
Is the generalization matrix symmetric? Why not?
"Not exactly. Training on a narrow template family gives you a direction that's tuned to that family's geometry, so it may not cover a broader test set. The combinations — like larger-than plus smaller-than — train on more variety and transfer better. Coverage of the training data matters more than the algorithm." 不对称来自训练集覆盖度。组合数据集（larger+smaller）迁移最好就是证据。
Did you run the original code, or reimplement it?
"We ran the original Marks-and-Tegmark repo on a DGX Spark with LLaMA-2-13B, and reproduced their headline numbers. The midterm additions — the negation splits, the label-free comparison, and the text baseline — are our own code on top of the same cached activations and the same split protocol." 原仓库复现 + 自写扩展，两层都要说到。same cached activations / same split 是严谨性的卖点。
What does this have to do with deceptive models and AI safety?
"Everything we've shown is about reading hidden semantics — what the model represents but doesn't say. A deceptive model is exactly the case where that gap matters. And notice which tool survived every test today: the label-free one. On the day you monitor for hidden intent, labels are precisely what you won't have." 把答案绕回两条主线：读"说了 vs 知道"的差距 + 无标签监控器。别承诺我们还没做的实验。
What exactly will you show in the final?
"Two things. Causality: push the truth direction into the residual stream and flip the model's answer — our early numbers move the logit gap from +0.12 to −0.09. And hidden knowledge: the Taboo model's secret word shows up in the top five predictions at layers seven to fifteen while the text never mentions it. Reading today, causing in December." 两个数字背熟：+0.12→−0.09、top-5 @ 7–15。收尾金句与场景 10 一致。
